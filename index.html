plantly.

On average, 50% of people routinely forget to water their house plants. On the other hand, overwatering is equally fatal: overwatering remains the leading cause of plant death globally.

The plantly. app bridges the divide between neglect and over hydration, seamlessly adding advanced tracking, interactive diagnostics, and refined gamification to elevate plant care standards.

Stage 01

Snap, Identify, & Confirm

Specific plants need specific needs, so the plant requires accurate classification. The user experience opens with a seamless interface that utilizes the camera to evaluate unique profiles.

  • AI Computer Vision: The ecosystem analyzes distinct patterns to instantly identify the correct botanical family.
  • User Verification: An intuitive check sequence confirms accuracy before loading custom hydration parameters.

Stage 02

Calibrated to Maturity

A developing sprout demands a profoundly different hydration routines than a fully matured, established plant.

  • Maturity Registration: Users smoothly input the approximate age of their plant directly into the digital profile.
  • Dynamic Scheduling: The algorithm recalibrates care criteria instantly. Delicate sprouts transition to lighter, frequent cycles, while mature roots receive deep, spaced intervals.

Stage 03

The Interactive Verification

Rather than sending standard alerts, Plantly operates as an intelligent digital assistant, requiring a short, real world diagnostic before any water is poured.

  • The Finger Test: The app guides users to insert a finger 1–2 inches deep into the topsoil to personally cross-reference hydration state.
  • Smart Dialogues: Confirming "moist soil" prompts the application assistant to strictly stop watering to avoid root rot and mold, while confirming "dry soil" safely authorizes hydration.
  • Frictionless Logging: A simple verification checklist securely timestamps the action, resetting the dynamic timer.

Stage 04

Cultivating Habits

To incentivize daily check ins and cultivate persistent wellness routines, plantly. uses gamification to turn the journey into an interactive journey.

  • The Goal: Preserving proper checkups and logging precise watering milestones over consistent days triggers growth to your mini companion
  • The Streak Companion: A digital pet germinates directly into the screen interface, growing and flourishing in sync with your physical houseplants.
  • The Tiers: Consistently providing your plant with proper care triggers different perks in the app to let you customize your plants, and your interface.
